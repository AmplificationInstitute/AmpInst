Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026 · Amplification Institute Inc.

Signal: Binaural Beats is built so there is as little to say in a privacy policy as possible. No accounts, no tracking, nothing leaves your device. This page explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Signal does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. If that ever changes — for example, if we add an optional analytics feature in the future — we will update this policy first and make it clearly optional.

Where your data lives

Your session settings and history are stored only on your device, using Apple's local storage. We never see this information, and it is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted along with it.

Purchases

If you make a purchase in Signal, that transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes your payment details, billing information, and Apple ID directly — Signal never receives, sees, or stores any of that information. Apple's own privacy practices govern that part of the process; see Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Children's privacy

Signal is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — because we don't collect information from anyone at all.

Your rights

Because Signal doesn't collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf — your data stays on your device, under your control, at all times. If you have questions about this, or believe this policy doesn't match the app's actual behavior, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If Signal's data practices ever change, we'll update this page and revise the date at the top before the change takes effect.
