The problem

Usually, technology isn't the problem.

The wrong process is being automated. The wrong tool is being selected. The economics don't work. Or the operating model, controls, adoption, and downstream implications haven't been thought through deeply enough.

01

Should we automate this at all?

Before selecting technology, test whether the process itself is worth preserving, redesigning, or eliminating.

02

Should we build, buy, or integrate?

Separate vendor promises from operating reality, architecture, controls, economics, and organizational fit.

03

Is this AI application actually ready for production?

Working software is not the same thing as production-ready software. Review the gaps before they become operating problems.

04

What are we missing?

Cost, controls, adoption, data, vendors, architecture, ownership, and workflow dependencies collide. The job is to see the whole picture.
